AI Policy

How we use AI.

A museum about the history of AI should be honest about its own use of it. Our policy rests on three principles.

Transparency

Any AI-generated content on this site is labelled “Generated by AI”.

Human verification

All information obtained with AI is checked by a person before it is published.

Responsible data management

  • Under Article 5 of the GDPR, only strictly necessary data is used by AI.
  • Under Article 13, the people concerned are told when AI is used to process their data.
  • Data is anonymised before it is submitted to an AI tool.
  • For each use, we publish what data is used, which model processes it and why.

Questions about this policy: hmai.contact@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
