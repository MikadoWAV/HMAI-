About Us

An archive for a technology we only picture in the future.

The Heritage Museum of Artificial Intelligence (HMAI) is a digital museum dedicated to the history and evolution of artificial intelligence. Once reserved for research labs, AI is now part of how we study, research, work and create.

Through digital immersion, the museum shows what different AI systems can do and invites visitors to reflect on the changes these technologies bring to modern society.

We welcome students and young adults curious about technology, and teachers, lecturers and researchers looking for a reliable educational resource.

Our missions

Mediate

Public talks and debates that demystify AI and tackle the myths and biases around it.

Catalogue

Document every kind of AI, starting with one landmark system for each.

Collaborate

Researchers and visitors can suggest a system to add to the archive.
