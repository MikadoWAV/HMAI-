A chronology of artificial intelligence.

Scroll through twenty milestones, from a 1943 paper to the models of today.

Gallery I · 1943 – 1997

Origins

  1. 1943

    The artificial neuron

    Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts publish the first mathematical model of an artificial neural network.

    McCulloch & Pitts
  2. 1950

    The Turing Test

    Alan Turing proposes judging a machine’s intelligence by its ability to pass as human in conversation.

    Alan Turing
  3. 1951

    SNARC

    The first neural network machine, built with 40 artificial neurons.

    Marvin Minsky & Dean Edmonds
  4. 1956

    The Dartmouth workshop

    The term artificial intelligence is used for the first time, at a summer research project.

    John McCarthy
  5. 1966

    ELIZA

    The first chatbot imitates a psychotherapist by rephrasing what users type.

    Joseph Weizenbaum
  6. 1970

    SHRDLU

    A program understands typed commands about a small world of blocks.

    Terry Winograd
  7. 1976

    MYCIN

    An expert system recommends treatments for bacterial infections.

    Stanford University
  8. 1997

    Deep Blue

    IBM’s computer defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

    IBM

Gallery II · 1987 – 2011

Learning

  1. 1987–93

    The AI winter

    Funding and interest collapse after promises go unmet.

    Research field
  2. 1998

    LeNet-5

    A convolutional network reads handwritten digits on bank cheques.

    Yann LeCun
  3. 2006

    Deep learning

    New training methods revive multi-layer neural networks.

    Geoffrey Hinton
  4. 2011

    Watson

    IBM’s question-answering system wins the quiz show Jeopardy!

    IBM
  5. 2011

    Siri

    A voice assistant arrives in millions of pockets with the iPhone 4S.

    Apple

Gallery III · 2012 – today

Generative

  1. 2012

    AlexNet

    A deep network wins the ImageNet competition and starts the deep learning boom.

    Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton
  2. 2014

    GAN

    Generative adversarial networks learn to produce new images.

    Ian Goodfellow
  3. 2016

    AlphaGo

    A program beats Go champion Lee Sedol, four games to one.

    DeepMind
  4. 2017

    The Transformer

    The paper “Attention Is All You Need” introduces the architecture behind modern language models.

    Google researchers
  5. 2018

    GPT-1

    The first generative pre-trained transformer.

    OpenAI
  6. 2020–26

    Large language models

    GPT-4 and GPT-5, Claude and Gemini bring conversational AI to the public.

    OpenAI, Anthropic, Google
  7. 2022–26

    Generative images and video

    DALL-E, Midjourney and Sora turn text into pictures and film.

    OpenAI, Midjourney

End of the timeline
