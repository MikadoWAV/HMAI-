Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

HMAI collects as little personal data as possible. This page explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have under the GDPR.

Data controller

Heritage Museum of Artificial Intelligence (HMAI), hmai.contact@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Bookings: email, visit type, date and number of visitors.
  • Contact messages: name, email and message.
  • Display preferences: dark mode and cookie choices, stored only in your browser.

Legal basis

  • Bookings: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Contact messages: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Optional cookies: consent only.

Retention

Booking data: 3 years after the visit. Contact messages: 12 months after our last reply.

Recipients

HMAI staff and our hosting and payment providers, bound by contract. We never sell your data. Data stays within the European Union.

Your rights

Access, correct, delete, restrict, port or object (Articles 15 to 22): write to hmai.contact@gmail.com. We answer within one month. You can complain to the CNIL.
